\chapter{Troubleshooting and Known Limitations}
\label{chap:troubleshooting-and-known-limitations}

This chapter is part of the new editorial skeleton for the user manual. It preserves the closing user chapter while the troubleshooting list is rebuilt from the live repository.

\noindent\textbf{Objective.} Summarize common issues, their expected symptoms, and the current limits of support.

\noindent\textbf{Audience.} Users, instructors, and operators who need a concise place to check failure modes and boundaries.

\noindent\textbf{Scope.} Cover the most visible errors, practical recovery steps, and the limitations that should be stated before the manual reaches a release candidate.

\noindent\textbf{Status.} Temporary skeleton only; the full troubleshooting matrix will be added later.
