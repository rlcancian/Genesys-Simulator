\chapter{Governance and Contribution}
\label{chap:governance-and-contribution}

This chapter is part of the new editorial skeleton for the developer manual. It preserves the governance chapter while the contribution policy is reorganized.

\noindent\textbf{Objective.} Explain the governance documents, contribution expectations, and review boundaries.

\noindent\textbf{Audience.} Contributors, maintainers, and reviewers who need to align their changes with project policy.

\noindent\textbf{Scope.} Cover the canonical documents, branch and review conventions, and the current human-decision boundaries.

\noindent\textbf{Status.} Temporary skeleton only; the final contribution guide will be expanded later.
