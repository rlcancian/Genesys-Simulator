\chapter{Results, Traces, and Reports}
\label{chap:results-traces-and-reports}

This chapter is part of the new editorial skeleton for the user manual. It keeps the results chapter in place while the reporting workflow is rebuilt.

\noindent\textbf{Objective.} Explain how users inspect traces, outputs, and simulation reports after a run.

\noindent\textbf{Audience.} Readers who need to interpret model output and compare runs.

\noindent\textbf{Scope.} Cover the user-visible result panels, textual traces, summary outputs, and the boundaries of what those outputs prove.

\noindent\textbf{Status.} Temporary skeleton only; the final reporting examples will be written later.
