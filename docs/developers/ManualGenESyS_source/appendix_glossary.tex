\chapter{Glossary}
\label{chap:appendix-glossary}

This glossary collects the terms that recur across the current manual. Each
term is indexed so the back matter can support navigation as well as reading.
\index{glossary}

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{|p{3.6cm}|p{8.0cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning in this manual} \\
\hline
Build evidence\index{build evidence} & Evidence that a named preset or build tree configures and compiles successfully. \\
\hline
Startup evidence\index{startup evidence} & Evidence that the executable opens, runs, and closes without a launch failure. \\
\hline
Functional evidence\index{functional evidence} & Evidence that a focused user flow or service path behaves as documented. \\
\hline
Numerical evidence\index{numerical evidence} & Evidence that a bounded numerical example reproduces the observed result for the documented inputs. \\
\hline
Scientific evidence\index{scientific evidence} & Evidence that the model interpretation was reviewed against a domain reference and an explicit validation basis. \\
\hline
Security evidence\index{security evidence} & Evidence that the documented security boundary was inspected without implying broader trust or public exposure. \\
\hline
Release readiness\index{release readiness} & The state in which the repository's validation, review, and governance gates all permit publication. \\
\hline
FIGURE-SPEC\index{FIGURE-SPEC} & A comment block that records the purpose, validation, and intended content of a figure placeholder. \\
\hline
Placeholder figure\index{figure!placeholder} & A compilable figure stub that stands in for the final artwork until a definitive asset exists. \\
\hline
Target\index{target} & The build-system name that identifies a compiled executable or library. \\
\hline
Executable\index{executable} & The runtime launcher name produced by the target. \\
\hline
Preset\index{preset} & A named configure or build entry in \texttt{CMakePresets.json}. \\
\hline
Local-only service\index{local-only service} & A service that the manual documents as private or loopback-oriented and not approved for public Internet exposure. \\
\hline
Whole-cell modeling\index{whole-cell modeling} & A biological modeling approach that aims to cover the molecular life cycle of a cell as a coupled system. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The glossary deliberately keeps the meanings narrow. If a later validated
chapter changes a term's scope, the change must appear in both the chapter text
and the glossary entry.
